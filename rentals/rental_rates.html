Rental Information & Rates

Rent the historic Fergus Theatre or one of our Bakken Building rehearsal spaces for performances, meetings, film screenings, special events, weddings, and more.

Request Rental Information

Spaces Available for Rent

A Center for the Arts offers several unique spaces for performances, rehearsals, meetings, educational programs, and special events.

Fergus Theatre

A 407-seat historic theatre available for performances, film screenings, meetings, weddings, and special events.

Large Rehearsal Space

A 1,200-square-foot rehearsal space with a piano, sound reinforcement, microphones, and a sprung floor.

Small Rehearsal Space

A 496-square-foot space with a piano, laminate flooring, and a dedicated carpeted area for youth activities.

Rental Requirements & Policies

$100 Date Deposit

A non-refundable $100 deposit is required to hold a requested rental date.

Reserve Early

A Center for the Arts maintains a busy schedule of performances, rehearsals, and classes and often plans events well in advance.

Required Personnel

Certain events require the use of AC4TA personnel, including House and Stage Management.
