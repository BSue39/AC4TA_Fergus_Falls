Frequently Asked Questions

Rental packages may include use of the selected venue spaces, standard seating, and access to available facility amenities. Specific inclusions depend on the type of event and rental agreement. Contact AC4TA for details about what is included with your rental.

We recommend contacting AC4TA as early as possible to discuss your preferred date and event needs. Availability can vary depending on the season, performances, and other scheduled activities.
