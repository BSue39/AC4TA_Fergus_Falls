Time Has Taken Its Toll

Years of performances, rehearsals, and community events have left our stage curtains worn, faded, and damaged.

Dry rot, tears, and age-related deterioration now threaten an essential part of the theatre experience.

Why It Matters

Every Performance Begins Behind The Curtain

New stage curtains do more than improve appearances. They frame every performance, protect our historic theatre, and help create unforgettable experiences for audiences, performers, and future generations.

Performance Quality

Professional stage curtains enhance lighting, acoustics, and every production.

Audience Experience

Beautiful curtains create anticipation and elevate every visit to the theatre.

Preserving Our Future

Investing today ensures decades of performances for our entire community.
