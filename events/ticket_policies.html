Ticket Information & Policies

Whether you're purchasing tickets for your first performance or you're a returning patron, we're here to make your visit simple, enjoyable, and stress-free. Review the information below before purchasing your tickets or attending an event.

Buy Online

Avoid the line. Purchase your tickets in advance for popular performances.

Box Office Hours

Tuesday–Friday
11:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Mobile Tickets

Present your ticket on your phone or bring a printed copy.

Purchasing Tickets

  • Tickets can be purchased online, by phone, or at the box office.
  • Advance ticket purchases are recommended for popular performances.
  • Seating may be assigned or general admission, depending on the event.
  • Ticket prices and availability vary by event.
  • There are no additional fees when purchasing online.

Refund & Exchange Policy

Tickets are not refundable.

All ticket sales are final unless an event is canceled by the presenter.

Ticket exchanges may be available before the performance, depending on the event and ticket availability.

Attending an Event

Arrival Time

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the performance begins.

Mobile Tickets

Printed and digital tickets are accepted for admission.

Accessibility

Accessible seating and accommodations are available upon request.

Late Seating

Late arrivals may be seated at an appropriate break in the performance, upon discretion of the theatre.

After You Purchase

Once your purchase is complete, you'll receive a confirmation email with your order details.

Your tickets may be printed at home or presented on your mobile device at the theater.

If you don't receive your confirmation email, please contact the box office for assistance.
