Getting Here

Located in the Heart of Downtown Fergus Falls

A Center for the Arts is conveniently located in historic downtown Fergus Falls, just steps from local restaurants, coffee shops, shopping, and community attractions. Whether you're traveling across town or visiting from out of the area, our location makes it easy to enjoy an evening of entertainment while exploring everything downtown has to offer.

Parking

Convenient Parking Options

Several convenient parking options are available within walking distance of A Center for the Arts, making it easy to arrive and enjoy your performance.

Street Parking

Free street parking is available throughout downtown Fergus Falls. Please observe posted parking signs and restrictions.

Public Parking Lots

Additional public parking lots are located just a short walk from the theater, providing convenient access during busy performances and community events.

Accessible Parking

Designated accessible parking spaces are available near the theater entrance for guests requiring accessible access.

Accessibility

Accessible for Every Guest

A Center for the Arts is committed to providing a welcoming and comfortable experience for all patrons. If you require accommodations or have accessibility questions before your visit, our staff is happy to assist.

Accessible Seating

Wheelchair-accessible seating is available throughout the theater. Please contact the box office when purchasing tickets to reserve accessible seating.

Assisted Listening

Assisted listening devices are available for select performances. Please inquire at the box office before the performance begins. ASL interpretation is also available upon prior arrangement.

Large Print Playbills

Large print playbills are available by request. Please contact the box office in advance of your performance to request a large print copy.

Accessible Facilities

Accessible entrances, restrooms, and other guest amenities are available to help ensure a comfortable visit for all patrons.

Day of Your Visit

Helpful Tips Before the Curtain Rises

A little preparation goes a long way. Keep these tips in mind to help ensure a smooth, relaxing, and enjoyable experience at A Center for the Arts.

Arrive Early

Plan to arrive at least 30 minutes before showtime to allow time for parking, ticket scanning, and finding your seat.

Have Your Tickets Ready

Printed tickets and mobile tickets are both accepted. Having them ready will help speed up entry.

Visit Concessions

Stop by the concession stand before the performance begins, as service may be limited during the show.

Silence Mobile Devices

Please silence phones and other electronic devices before the performance begins out of courtesy to performers and fellow guests.

Late Arrival

Guests arriving after the performance has started may be seated during an appropriate break, depending on the production.
