Something for Everyone

A Center for the Arts offers a variety of community events and programs that are free to attend or offered through freewill donation.

Community Program

Voices Rise

A welcoming choral experience for individuals living with memory loss and their care partner, as well as other “Choral Companions.”

Voices Rise brings participants together through singing and shared musical experiences in a welcoming community environment.

More Info & Registration
Theatre Organ Concerts

Brown Bag Lunch

Enjoy live theatre-organ music during the annual Brown Bag Lunch Concert Series at A Center for the Arts.

Concerts take place at noon, and admission is offered through freewill donation.

Theatre Organ

The Mighty Wurlitzer

A Center for the Arts is home to a 3/12 Wurlitzer theatre pipe organ installed at the Center in 1994.

The Wurlitzer is featured in theatre-organ concerts and other special programming throughout the year.

Our Mission

The mission of A Center for the Arts in Fergus Falls is to connect artists, patrons and community by providing the best possible arts experiences that inspire creativity, curiosity, imagination, and learning.
