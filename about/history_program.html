The Theatre Is Saved

Community volunteers and supporters rallied together to preserve the historic theatre and restore live performances to downtown Fergus Falls.

Wurlitzer Installation

The installation of the Wurlitzer theatre pipe organ added a unique musical experience to the theatre and established it as a destination for organ enthusiasts and performers.

Grand Reopening

The theatre celebrated its grand reopening, welcoming the community back through its doors and beginning a new chapter of performances, events, and arts experiences.

Auditorium Renovation

Improvements to the auditorium helped preserve the theatre's historic character while creating a more comfortable and welcoming environment for audiences and performers.

Regional Arts Hub

Today, A Center for the Arts serves as a regional destination for performances, arts education, community programs, and cultural experiences that bring people together.

Key Milestones

1982

Community Theatre Returns

The Old Tyme Variety Players begin using the space, marking the return of live theatre to the building and laying the groundwork for what would eventually become A Center for the Arts.

1995

Grand Reopening

Following a community fundraising effort exceeding $1 million, the theatre reopens as a fully renovated, year-round arts facility with updated accessibility, backstage areas, and audience amenities.

2000s

Education Expansion

Youth theatre and arts education programs expand, providing opportunities for young people to develop confidence, creativity, and stage experience through participation in productions, classes, and workshops.

2010s

Youth Theatre Growth

Youth theatre becomes a cornerstone of AC4TA's programming, with hundreds of young performers participating in annual productions, classes, and workshops that foster creativity, teamwork, and confidence.

Today

Regional Arts Hub

AC4TA serves thousands of visitors annually through touring performances, community theatre, youth programs, arts education, concerts, film screenings, and special events that connect people to the arts and each other.
