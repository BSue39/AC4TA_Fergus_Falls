History of the Building

Preserving A Landmark

For more than a century, this historic theater has stood as a gathering place for entertainment, culture, and community.


Explore the Building

From Orpheum Theatre To Arts Center

Originally opened in 1921, the theater served generations of audiences through live performances, motion pictures, and community events.

Today it remains one of the region's most important historic cultural landmarks.

Did You Know?

  • The building originally opened as the Orpheum Theatre in 1921.

  • Thousands of volunteer hours helped preserve the building.
  • The Wurlitzer organ was installed decades after the theater opened.
