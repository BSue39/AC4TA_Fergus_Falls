Our Mission

Bringing People Together Through Creative Experiences

A Center for the Arts enriches the quality of life in our region by providing access to performing arts, visual arts, arts education, and cultural opportunities for all ages.

Our Vision

To be the cultural heart of west-central Minnesota, inspiring creativity, fostering connection, and ensuring access to the arts for future generations.
