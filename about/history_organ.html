Meet The Organ

A Historic Wurlitzer Theatre Organ

Installed in the 1990s, the Geneva Eschweiler Wurlitzer transformed the theater into a destination for organ enthusiasts. The theatre organ was designed to bring silent films to life, providing music, sound effects, and dramatic accompaniment for audiences before motion pictures included synchronized sound.

Today, the organ remains one of the most treasured features of the theatre and continues to be showcased during special performances and community events.

Key Milestones

1994

Installation Begins

The Geneva Eschweiler Theatre Pipe Organ is installed, marking a major milestone in the theatre's restoration and establishing a new identity as a regional arts destination with a unique musical attraction.

1995

Dedication Concert

The Mighty Wurlitzer is officially dedicated with a concert by renowned organist Mark Herman, drawing a large audience and generating excitement for the theatre's future programming.

2005

Restoration Work

Ongoing maintenance and restoration efforts are undertaken to preserve the organ's sound quality and mechanical reliability, ensuring it remains a cherished part of the theatre experience for years to come.

2024

Ongoing Maintenance

Regular tuning, cleaning, and repairs are performed by a dedicated team of volunteers and technicians, helping to keep the organ in top condition and ensuring it continues to delight audiences for generations to come.
